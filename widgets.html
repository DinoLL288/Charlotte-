On your home screen

Phone widgets

Our mood and bathroom trackers, right on your iPhone or Mac home screen.

How it works

A website on its own can't make iPhone or Mac widgets — those need an app. So we use a free widget app: Scriptable on iPhone / iPad, and ScriptWidget on Mac (Scriptable isn't available on macOS). The widgets read a tiny public summary from our site (latest moods and today's bathroom counts, no notes or history), and tapping one opens the matching tracker page.

Set them up — iPhone / iPad (Scriptable)

  1. Install Scriptable free from the App Store.
  2. In Scriptable, tap + to make a new script and give it a name, like “Mood”.
  3. Tap Copy above for the widget you want, then paste the whole script into Scriptable and save it.
  4. Long-press the home screen → + → Scriptable → pick a widget size → long-press the widget → Edit Widget → choose your script.
  5. Tap the widget any time to open the tracker on our site.

Set them up — Mac (ScriptWidget)

  1. Install ScriptWidget free from scriptwidget.app and open ScriptWidget Studio.
  2. In Studio, choose Blank Widget and give it a name, like “Mood” (repeat for “Bathroom”).
  3. Tap Copy script above, then paste the whole script over main.jsx. Tap Copy manifest and paste that over widget.json — the manifest lets the widget reach our site, which the fetch needs.
  4. Press Command-R to preview and Command-S to save.
  5. Add the widget from the system widget gallery and pick the widget you just made.
  6. Tap the widget any time to open the tracker on our site.

Widgets refresh on your device's own schedule, so they can be a little behind — tapping one always opens the latest on the site.

The site itself, on your home screen too

On iPhone, open our site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen — it sits next to the widgets like an app. On Android, use Chrome's menu → Add to Home screen.
