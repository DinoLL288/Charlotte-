On your home screen

Phone widgets

Our mood and bathroom trackers, right on your iPhone or Mac home screen.

How it works

A website on its own can't make iPhone or Mac widgets — those need an app. So we use Scriptable, a free app that runs little JavaScript widgets. The widgets read a tiny public summary from our site (latest moods and today's bathroom counts, no notes or history), and tapping one opens the matching tracker page.

Set them up

  1. Install Scriptable free from the App Store (iPhone / iPad) or the Mac App Store (Mac).
  2. In Scriptable, tap + to make a new script and give it a name, like “Mood”.
  3. Tap Copy above for the widget you want, then paste the whole script into Scriptable and save it.
  4. On iPhone: long-press the home screen → + → Scriptable → pick a widget size → long-press the widget → Edit Widget → choose your script. On Mac: add it from Notification Center instead.
  5. Tap the widget any time to open the tracker on our site.

Widgets refresh on your phone's own schedule, so they can be a little behind — tapping one always opens the latest on the site.

The site itself, on your home screen too

On iPhone, open our site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen — it sits next to the widgets like an app. On Android, use Chrome's menu → Add to Home screen.
